# Offer specification — source of truth

Any number, term or claim about our offer that appears on the website must match this file.
If the site and this file disagree, this file wins. Never invent a rate, term or guarantee
that is not written here.

## Business model

We place experienced Filipino professionals as independent contractors with US businesses,
and manage the match. The client pays us monthly in advance; we pay the contractor. The
professional is not an employee of the client and not a payroll liability. The client
directs the work day to day. We handle sourcing, vetting, matching, onboarding, invoicing
and ongoing support.

## Rate card (USD per month)

A full-time month is 160 hours. A part-time month is 80 hours.

| Role                           | Part-time | Full-time | Project rate/hr |
| ------------------------------ | --------- | --------- | --------------- |
| Executive Assistant            | $960      | $1,760    | $14             |
| Social Media Manager           | $1,120    | $2,080    | $16             |
| Digital Marketing Professional | $1,360    | $2,560    | $19             |
| GoHighLevel & CRM Specialist   | $1,760    | $3,360    | $25             |

Scoped projects (CRM builds, GoHighLevel onboarding, automation setup) are quoted per
outcome, from $1,500.

Four factors decide which rate applies: specialization, experience, hours, engagement type.

## Engagement types

- Part-time support — 80 hours/month, consistent weekly hours on selected responsibilities.
- Full-time support — 160 hours/month, a dedicated professional in the client's working hours.
- Scoped project — priced per outcome, from $1,500, with documentation and handover.

## Payment terms

- On acceptance: $500 role activation fee, credited in full against the first monthly
  invoice. It is not an extra cost.
- Before the start date: first month, in advance — after the client has interviewed and
  chosen their professional.
- Monthly thereafter: one month in advance, same date each month, card or ACH auto-debit.
- Scoped projects: 50% deposit, 50% on handover.
- 30 days' written notice to end an engagement, either side, no exit fee.
- Rates quoted in a proposal are valid for 14 days.

The refund condition, which must always appear alongside the activation fee and never
separately: "If we cannot put at least two candidates in front of you that you are willing
to interview within 14 days, the activation fee is refunded in full."

## Included in the monthly rate

Sourcing, screening and shortlisting for the specific role; experience and reference
verification; written and spoken English assessment; matching to the client's tools and way
of working; onboarding support through the first 30 days; ongoing support and a route to
raise problems with us; contractor invoicing, payments and administration.

## Not included

Employment, benefits or statutory contributions (the professional is an independent
contractor); software licences and subscriptions; work outside the agreed scope or hours;
equipment beyond the professional's own workstation and connection; any guarantee of a
specific business outcome or revenue result.

## Process

1. Consultation
2. Role definition — written up and sent for client sign-off
3. Talent matching — at least two candidates within 14 days
4. Interview and selection — the client decides, and can decline and ask for others
5. Onboarding and support

## Claims we do NOT make

- No guarantee of a business outcome, revenue result or ROI figure.
- No claim that a professional will never leave.
- No unconditional replacement guarantee. Replacement support is set out in each client's
  services agreement. Do not describe it as a guarantee on the website.
- No named client testimonials, logos or case studies. We do not have any yet.
- No specific savings percentage unless shown as an illustrative comparison with its
  assumptions visible.

## Voice

Calm, plain, specific, understated. Short sentences. Contractions are fine. We say what
something is and what it costs. No hype, no urgency, no exclamation marks, and never words
like "unlock", "supercharge", "game-changing" or "10x". Where something is uncertain we say
so. Existing lines that set the tone: "No pressure. Just a 30-minute conversation about
your needs." and "The honest answer is it depends."
