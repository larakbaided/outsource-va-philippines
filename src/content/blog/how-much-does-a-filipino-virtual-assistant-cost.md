It's the first question almost every business owner asks: *how much does a Filipino virtual assistant actually cost?*

The honest answer is that it depends — but that's not a cop-out. The cost of a [virtual assistant](/services) varies for specific, understandable reasons. Once you know what drives the number, the published rate makes sense. Here's the full picture, and our card at the end of it.

## What actually drives the cost

Four factors move the price more than anything else:

1. **Specialization.** A general administrative VA costs less than a specialist. A [GoHighLevel virtual assistant](/services/gohighlevel-virtual-assistant), a marketing-automation expert, or an experienced executive assistant sits higher, because the skill is harder to find.
2. **Experience.** A professional with several years of hands-on work commands more than someone starting out — and usually needs far less hand-holding.
3. **Hours.** A part-time month is {{partTimeHours}} hours. A full-time month is {{fullTimeHours}}. More hours means a better effective rate per hour.
4. **Engagement type.** Ongoing support is billed monthly. A scoped project is quoted per outcome. They're priced in different ways.

## The common pricing models

You'll generally encounter cost quoted in one of four ways:

- **Hourly** — you pay for time worked. Flexible, and good for variable workloads.
- **Part-time retainer** — a set number of hours a month for a fixed fee. Predictable, and the most common choice for ongoing support.
- **Full-time** — a dedicated professional working your full schedule. The best effective rate per hour, suited to a steady workload.
- **Project-based** — a fixed scope for a defined deliverable, such as a [CRM setup or migration](/services/crm-automation). You pay for the outcome, not the hours.

## Why the cheapest option is rarely the cheapest

It's tempting to sort by lowest rate and pick the bottom of the list. In practice, that's often the most expensive route:

- **Rework costs more than the savings.** A bargain hire who needs everything corrected can cost you more in your own time than a capable professional would have charged outright.
- **Turnover is expensive.** Re-hiring and re-onboarding every few months burns time and momentum.
- **Mistakes in the wrong place hurt.** A misconfigured automation or a botched CRM migration can cost real revenue.

The goal isn't the lowest rate — it's the **best value**: someone experienced enough to work independently and get it right the first time.

## Freelance vs. agency: what you're paying for

A freelancer's headline rate can look lower because you're absorbing the hidden work yourself — screening candidates, vetting skills, onboarding, and carrying the risk if it doesn't work out. A [managed partnership](/why-hire-filipino-virtual-assistants) folds that work into the price: the professional is pre-vetted, matched to your needs, and supported over time. That's the fifth thing that moves the number, and for many owners the trade is worth it.
