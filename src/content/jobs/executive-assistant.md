## About the role

You'll support one US-based founder or executive as their right hand — protecting their calendar, staying on top of their inbox, and making sure nothing quietly falls through.

This suits someone who likes bringing order to a busy week and can make sensible judgment calls without checking in on every one.

## What you'll do

- Manage the calendar: scheduling, rescheduling, protecting focus time
- Triage the inbox, draft replies, and surface what genuinely needs attention
- Coordinate travel, bookings and expenses
- Prepare agendas, take notes, and follow up on actions
- Keep files, folders and internal documentation organised
- Handle personal admin where it overlaps with work

## What we're looking for

- Prior experience supporting an executive, founder or senior manager
- Excellent written English — you'll be writing on someone else's behalf
- Sound discretion with confidential information
- Anticipates rather than waits to be asked
- A reliable workstation and internet connection

## Nice to have

- Experience with US-based clients and time zones
- Light bookkeeping or invoicing exposure
- Project management tool experience

## How this works

This is an independent contractor engagement, not employment. You'd be placed with a client business that directs your day-to-day work, while we handle the matching, onboarding, invoicing and ongoing support. We pay you; you don't chase the client for payment.
