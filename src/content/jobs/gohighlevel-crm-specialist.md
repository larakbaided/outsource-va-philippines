## About the role

You'll work inside clients' GoHighLevel accounts as their specialist — building the workflows, pipelines and funnels that their business runs on, then keeping them working as things change.

Most of our clients are US-based agencies, coaches and service businesses. You'll be matched with one or two of them rather than juggling a queue of tickets, so you get to know their business properly.

## What you'll do

- Build and maintain workflows, pipelines, triggers and automations
- Set up funnels, forms, calendars and campaigns
- Migrate data and configure snapshots for new accounts
- Keep tags, custom fields and pipeline stages tidy and consistent
- Troubleshoot when an automation misfires, and explain what happened
- Document what you build so the client's team can follow it

## What we're looking for

- Hands-on GoHighLevel experience — you've built workflows from scratch, not just clicked through someone else's
- A methodical approach to testing before you ship
- Clear written English; you'll be explaining technical things to non-technical owners
- Comfortable working independently and flagging problems early
- A reliable workstation and internet connection

## Nice to have

- Agency or sub-account experience across multiple clients
- Zapier, Make or API integration work
- Basic HTML/CSS for funnel tweaks

## How this works

This is an independent contractor engagement, not employment. You'd be placed with a client business that directs your day-to-day work, while we handle the matching, onboarding, invoicing and ongoing support. We pay you; you don't chase the client for payment.
